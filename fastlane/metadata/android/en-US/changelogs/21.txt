The terminal's key bar is now a widget file, keybar.owl, which you or an agent can edit: its rows, keys and latches, with a key that can type a command, and its gaps and border in settings. This fork's own keys.toml is gone, replaced by this, and the bar it ships is the same one, so nothing looks different. Also: tokens kept in Android's keystore, password fields, PUT and PATCH requests, and the store no longer crashes when you open its panels tab.
